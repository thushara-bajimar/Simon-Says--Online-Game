How to Play?

🟢 The game starts by flashing a color. Click the flashed color to move to the next level.
🟡 At Level 2, a new color flashes. Click the previous color first, followed by the newly flashed color.
🔵 As the levels increase, one new color is added to the sequence. Repeat the entire sequence in the correct order, then click the newest flashed color.
🔴 If you make a mistake, the game ends, your score is displayed, and you can restart to try again!
